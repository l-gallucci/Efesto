# Transport-profile development audit — 9 November 2026

This is a development dataset and result snapshot, not an extension of the runtime HMM library. No MntX/Lut/LanA/Vup model is activated by this directory. The shipped catalog remains 663 registered / 495 enabled. The candidate HMM has no validated external physiological specificity cutoff.

The protocol combines reference-anchored phylogenetic curation with grouped nested HMM calibration. The MntX pilot uses three experimentally linked positive lineages and 133 targeted background controls, including an experimentally characterized ArsP-related control. Each outer evaluation excludes complete groups; inner out-of-fold predictions choose cutoffs by MCC. Alignment and HMM construction are repeated from training sequences only. Exact aliases are collapsed. The 70% and 90% sensitivity analyses independently recompute similarity links and retain conservative genus grouping of background controls. Linclust is an approximate grouping method, not proof that all remote homologs have been separated.

MntX outer predictions are 3 TP, 0 FN, 0 FP, 133 TN at all three grouping settings. These are sparse, targeted development results, not population accuracy. The 95% Wilson range for 3/3 independent positive lineages is approximately 0.439–1.000. With no observed errors, bootstrap intervals cannot establish an absence of unseen error. No independent external positive test exists. Candidate final cutoffs come from development only; final-build rescoring of development members is never an independent test. Official PF11449 baseline overlaps its source seed references.

The family snapshot contains 757 records including the three anchors. Official PF11449 recognizes 757, the three-anchor candidate 364, at different profile-specific thresholds. Reference-derived fragment diagnostics recover 24/27 and 18/27, respectively. These counts do not establish improved candidate specificity. Exploratory phylogenies use 80 MntX-related and 64 TonB-related sequences, genus-balanced subsets of broader snapshots, MAFFT, columns with less than 50% gaps and IQ-TREE LG+G4 with 1,000 SH-aLRT/UFBoot replicates. Nonstandard residues were excluded from exploratory tree selection; source sequences were not rewritten. No EPA-ng placement or proGenomes3 expansion was executed.

LutH references AM1 C5B163 and PA1 ABY30252.1 share 836/839 residues (99.64%). With OB3b A0A2D2CVQ0, only two independent groups remain. LanA has one independent characterized group; its 732-aa published query and 737-aa record are not independent positives. LutAEF have one verified experimental locus group. A generic TonB domain recognizes all four selected Ln references and 44/45 other annotated receptors; it is not an Ln-specific classifier. Nested validation is not claimed for these undersupported targets.

Historical Vup proteins map to Ava_4040 (binding component / functional VupA), Ava_4039 (permease / functional VupB) and Ava_4038 (ATPase / VupC). Historical A/B accession labels are inconsistent with this assignment; the unique HpaI site used in the source mutant lies in historical Avar03003112, the permease. This is a sequence/genomic reconstruction, not an author-issued correction. The three subunits in one genome are not three independent positive examples of one class. The tested Vup subunits do not pass the source Tup GA cutoffs.

Manifests retain raw sequence hashes and experimental/inferred/unlabelled evidence. Homologs retrieved by a broad family query were not turned into functional positives. Raw source URL/hash snapshots are indexed in source_downloads.json. Detailed local conclusions remain in the Git-ignored audit report.
