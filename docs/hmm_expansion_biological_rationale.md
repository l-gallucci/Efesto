# Biological scope

Efesto reports protein-family and pathway-component potential. The annotations
below are useful layers of genome interpretation, not direct measurements of
metal turnover or resistance.

| Layer | Useful annotation | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| Iron redox | Candidate electron-transfer and respiratory components | Shared multiheme/respiratory families can serve other pathways; direction and physiology need context |
| Metal acquisition | Binding proteins, permeases and transporter-associated families | A generic ATPase or TonB motor does not establish the substrate |
| Siderophore / metallophore synthesis | Biosynthetic families and contextual combinations | Family matches need not resolve product identity, secretion or metal use |
| Heme handling | Transport, binding and degradation components | Different stages and shared-substrate families must remain distinct |
| Metal resistance | Families associated with detoxification or export | Gene presence is not a measured resistance phenotype or clinical classification |
| Fe-S biogenesis | Cofactor-assembly and transfer machinery | Not a diagnosis of iron starvation or iron respiration |
| Lanthanide handling | LanM/LanD binding and chaperone-related potential | Not a complete uptake pathway or proof of lanthanide-dependent growth |
| Mo/W uptake | Wtp/Tup-related components | Shared substrates and model-specific coverage gaps remain |

## Current additions

The official Ln/W and Mbn profiles are described in [metal expansion](metal_expansion.md).
The [Fe-S page](iron_sulfur_assembly.md) lists the actual assembly models.
Models and categories in those pages are tied to the registry; older proposals
and example accessions are not a deployment list.

## Candidates under development

MntX, LutH/LanA, LutAEF, Vup and methylolanthanin require additional specificity
work. Other metallophore candidates, including PDTC and delftibactin, require
product/locus-level evidence before assigning a metal-specific process. A generic
NRPS or transporter profile would not provide that specificity.

Follow the [development status](development.md), [methodology](hmm_methodology.md)
and [validation results](validation.md). No function is enabled solely because it
appears as a candidate in the research roadmap.
